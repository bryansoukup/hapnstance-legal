# Hapnstance Support

Need help with Hapnstance?

Email **bryan.soukup@gmail.com** (also under You → Email in the app).

Hapnstance is a live crowd map for public places. It is not a dating app. There are no profiles, photos, swipes, chat, or matches.

A pin lights when at least two people nearby have Hapnstance open at the same public place. Quiet is honest. We never invent a crowd.

Location is only used while the app is open. Sharing pauses when you leave.

Hapnstance+ is an optional App Store subscription. Manage or cancel it in Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Venues: book a sponsored night ($79 for 7 days) at [Venues](venues.html). It is paid on the website via Stripe, not through Apple.
