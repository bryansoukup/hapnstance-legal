# Hapnstance Terms

You must be 18 or older to use Hapnstance.

Hapnstance helps you see where people are gathering in public. It does not introduce you, message anyone, or verify anyone's identity. It is not a dating service. Dating is an optional crowd filter. We show you the room. You walk over.

Do not use the app to find or visit private residences. Do not stalk, harass, or attempt to identify individuals from crowd data.

Crowd counts are aggregate and can be wrong or empty. Quiet is honest. Service is provided as-is.

Hapnstance+ is an optional auto-renewing subscription billed through Apple on iPhone. Yearly is $39.99 per year ($3.33 per month) with a 7-day free trial. Monthly is $6.99 per month. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. The subscription renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Unused trial time is forfeited when you buy.

Sponsored nights are paid listings from public venues ($79 for 7 days), booked on the Hapnstance website. They are not sold in the iPhone app and are not an Apple in-app purchase. They are ads, not live crowd counts, and they are not introductions. A venue must pass a public-listing handshake before a night can be posted. Book and pay at https://bryansoukup.github.io/hapnstance-legal/venues.html (payments processed by Stripe). If a venue does not pass verification, the payment is refunded in full.

If you do not agree, do not use the app.
