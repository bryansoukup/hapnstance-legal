# Hapnstance Terms

You must be 18 or older to use Hapnstance.

Hapnstance helps you see where people are gathering in public. It does not introduce you, message anyone, or verify anyone's identity. It is not a dating service. Dating is an optional crowd filter. We show you the room. You walk over.

Do not use the app to find or visit private residences. Do not stalk, harass, or attempt to identify individuals from crowd data.

Crowd counts are aggregate and can be wrong or empty. Quiet is honest. Service is provided as-is.

Hapnstance+ is an optional auto-renewing subscription billed through Apple on iPhone. Yearly is $39.99 per year ($3.33 per month) with a 7-day free trial where offered. Monthly is $6.99 per month. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. The subscription renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Unused trial time is forfeited when you buy.

Sponsored nights are paid listings from public venues ($79 for 7 days), booked and paid on the Hapnstance website via Stripe, not through Apple. They are not an Apple in-app purchase; in the US, the app only links to the website. They are ads, not live crowd counts, and they are not introductions. A venue must pass a public-listing handshake before a night can be posted. Book at https://bryansoukup.github.io/hapnstance-legal/venues.html. If a venue does not pass verification, the payment is refunded in full.

Zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Scene notes and vibe tags must not contain harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, or anyone's personal information, and must not be used to single out a person. Every note has Report and Block, and every sponsored listing has Report. Report hides the item for everyone right away, flags it for review, and blocks its anonymous author: their other notes and vibe tags are hidden and that phone cannot post them for 30 days. Block also hides that author's notes and vibe tags on your phone right away; you can clear your blocked list under You. Content that breaks these rules is removed. Contact bryan.soukup@gmail.com to report anything else.

If you do not agree, do not use the app.
