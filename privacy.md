# Hapnstance Privacy Policy

Hapnstance is a real-world crowd map. It is not a dating app, chat app, or profile directory. We do not sell your data. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## What we use

- Location, only while the app is open, to count you at a nearby public place.
- Age, gender, orientation, interests, and why you are out - to filter aggregate crowd layers you choose to see.
- Anonymous scene notes and vibe tags you choose to post.
- Optional swarm links you create. A swarm is a 4-hour token for one public pin. No names.
- Venue-night claims: we store that a session verified a public place, plus the public website domain. We do not store the email or phone you typed.

## What we never show

- Your name, photo, or profile.
- A pin on you.
- A private home as a hotspot, even if people there have the app open.

## How heat works

You only count toward a place if you are within about 75 meters of a public accommodation (bar, cafe, restaurant, park, venue, and similar). Residences are excluded. Your GPS is never drawn as a pin.

## Retention

Sharing pauses when you leave the app or turn location off. Your live count is removed as soon as we can tell you have left, and otherwise within about 90 seconds. Scene notes expire after a few hours. Swarm links expire after 4 hours. Sponsored nights expire after 7 days.

## Purchases

Hapnstance+ is processed by Apple. We do not receive your full Apple ID or card number.

You can turn location off or close the app at any time. There is no account to delete.

## Contact

Report a note in the app. For anything else, use the contact listed on the Hapnstance App Store page.
