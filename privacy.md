# Hapnstance Privacy Policy

Hapnstance is a real-world crowd map. It is not a dating app, chat app, or profile directory. We do not sell your data. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## What we use

- Location, only while the app is open, to count you at a nearby public place. If you do not allow location, you can still browse the map around the home ZIP you entered, and you are never counted anywhere.
- Age, gender, orientation, interests, and why you are out - to filter aggregate crowd layers you choose to see.
- Anonymous scene notes and vibe tags you choose to post. Each is stored with a one-way hash of the app's random session id (never your name, Apple ID, or device identifier) so that a report can hide that author's other notes and pause their posting. Notes and vibe tags are shown with a separate opaque author key so Block can hide one author's posts on your phone; your list of blocked authors stays on your phone. They expire after about 3 hours.
- Optional swarm links you create. A swarm is a 4-hour token for one public pin. No names.
- Venue claims: we store the public place, its website domain, the role you pick, and an optional short note. We never ask for or store email or phone numbers.
- Crowd server: while you count at a public place we store a random anonymous session id with that place and the crowd traits above. It is deleted about 90 minutes after your last check-in, or right away when you turn off Show me on the map. Crowd traits are only shared when at least two people are at the same public place. We do not store names, IP addresses, or device identifiers in the crowd database; our host may keep standard short-lived request logs.

## What we never show

- Your name, photo, or profile.
- A pin on you.
- A private home as a hotspot, even if people there have the app open.

## How heat works

You only count toward a place if you are within about 75 meters of a public accommodation (bar, cafe, restaurant, park, venue, and similar). Residences are excluded. Your GPS is never drawn as a pin.

## Retention

Once you count at a public place, you stay counted for about 90 minutes after your last check-in, even if you pocket the phone, walk away, turn location off, or GPS jumps. Turning off Show me on the map removes you right away (on the next sync, within about 20 seconds). Opening the app at home does not count, and a private home never appears. Scene notes and vibe tags expire after about 3 hours. Swarm links expire after 4 hours. Sponsored nights expire 7 days after they go live.

## Purchases

Hapnstance+ is processed by Apple. We do not receive your full Apple ID or card number.

Venues that book a sponsored night on the website pay through Stripe. Stripe processes the card; we receive the venue contact email, phone, and the venue and night details entered at checkout, and use them only to verify the venue and run the listing. App users never pay through Stripe.

You can turn location off or close the app at any time. There is no account to delete.
