Sponsored Nights for Venues

Hapnstance is a live map of public nights. Venues can put one real night on the map as a Sponsored Night.

Book a sponsored night — $79
Secure checkout by Stripe. One-time payment, not a subscription.

What you get

  • One real night at your public venue, shown on the Hapnstance map for 7 days.
  • $79 one-time, USD.
  • Your night is clearly labeled SPONSORED. It is an ad.

What it never does

  • It never changes live crowd counts. Crowd heat only comes from people actually there. Quiet is honest.
  • It is not an introduction, message, or match service.

Who can book

  • Public venues only: bars, cafes, restaurants, music venues, parks, and similar places open to the public.
  • No private homes or private residences, ever.

Verification handshake

Before a night goes live, Hapnstance verifies that your venue is a real public place (the public-listing handshake). This is done within 1 business day of payment. At checkout you give the venue name, the venue address or city, and the night date and title, plus a contact email and phone.

Refunds

If your venue does not pass verification, your night is not posted and your $79 is refunded in full.

Why this is on the website

Sponsored nights are booked and paid here on the Hapnstance website. They are not sold in the iPhone app and are not an Apple in-app purchase.
